# ***Mathematical Expressions*** 

The following mathematical equations are used in my **Black Hole Simulation Project**:

*Simulation of light rays moving through the curved spacetime around a Schwarzschild black hole. Rays are launched from configurable emitter presets, integrated with a Runge–Kutta 4 (RK4) solver, and rendered in real time with impact-parameter-based coloring, fading motion trails, and reference circles for the event horizon, photon sphere, and ISCO.*

### 1. Schwarzschild Radius

$$
r_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2}
$$

This equation is used to calculate the **Schwarzschild radius** of the black hole.

### 2. Critical Impact Parameter

$$
b_{\text{crit}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}r_s
$$

This equation is used in the simulation to calculate the **critical impact parameter** for the light rays.

### 3. Radial Motion of Light

$$
\left(\frac{dr}{d\lambda}\right)^2
=
E^2-\left(1-\frac{r_s}{r}\right)\frac{L^2}{r^2}
$$

This equation is used to determine the **radial motion of the light rays** around the black hole.

### 4. Angular Motion

$$
\frac{d\phi}{d\lambda}=\frac{L}{r^2}
$$

This equation is used to calculate the **angular movement of the light rays**.
